**September 2023 – Present | Nagpur, India**

Working on a business accounting and invoicing platform serving thousands of SME users.

#### Key Contributions

- 🚀 Built **five features from scratch**, including multi-currency invoicing, credit notes, customer categories, product categories, and invoice image attachments.
- 💱 Implemented **multi-currency invoicing** with manual exchange-rate entry and automated gain/loss calculations.
